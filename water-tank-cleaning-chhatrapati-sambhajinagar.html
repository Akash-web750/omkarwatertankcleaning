Professional Water Tank Cleaning Process We Follow

Our water tank cleaning process is carried out according to the tank type, condition, size, and site requirements. The process focuses on removing accumulated water, sludge, sediment, dirt, and other deposits from the tank before cleaning, sanitisation, UV treatment, and final inspection.

1. Tank Inspection & Preparation

We first inspect the water tank and assess its condition, size, access, and visible deposits before starting the cleaning work. The cleaning approach is then planned according to the tank and site requirements.

2. Water Removal

Stored water is removed from the tank as required so that the internal floor and surfaces can be accessed for cleaning.

3. Sludge & Sediment Removal

Accumulated sludge, mud, sediment, dirt, and other deposits are removed from the bottom and internal areas of the tank.

4. Internal Cleaning & Manual Scrubbing

The internal surfaces of the tank are cleaned and manually scrubbed to remove deposits and dirt from the tank floor and walls.

5. Water Washing & High-Pressure Cleaning

The tank's internal surfaces are washed with water to remove remaining dirt and loosened deposits. Where required, suitable pressure-based cleaning is used according to the tank condition and cleaning requirements.

6. Sanitisation

After the internal cleaning and washing stages, the tank is sanitised as part of the cleaning process to support hygienic water storage.

7. UV / UV Light Treatment

UV light treatment is included as part of the applicable cleaning and treatment process. UV treatment is used as an additional treatment step alongside the physical cleaning and sanitisation process.

8. Final Inspection & Preparation for Use

The tank is inspected after cleaning and treatment to check the cleaned internal surfaces and overall condition before it is prepared for regular water storage and use.

The exact cleaning method can vary depending on whether the tank is overhead or underground, its construction and storage capacity, its condition, and the site requirements.

Professional water tank cleaning helps remove accumulated sludge, sediment, algae, dirt, and other deposits from the tank's internal surfaces. How the Climate of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Affects Water Storage Tanks

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar experiences hot summer conditions, seasonal monsoon rainfall, and dusty conditions during parts of the year. These environmental factors can increase the amount of dust, leaves, insects, and other debris entering poorly protected overhead water tanks, particularly when tank lids are damaged, loose, or not properly sealed. Warm conditions and stagnant water can also create conditions that encourage algae and microbial growth when storage tanks are not regularly inspected and maintained.

During the monsoon, rain, leaves, insects, dirt, and organic particles may enter tanks that are not properly protected. In underground tanks, sediment and heavier particles can gradually settle at the bottom and form sludge deposits. Regular inspection and professional cleaning help remove accumulated deposits and allow property owners to identify issues such as damaged lids, blocked outlets, or excessive sediment before they become more difficult to manage.
